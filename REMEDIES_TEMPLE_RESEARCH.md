## A. Life-aspect / deity temples

### Marriage (timely marriage, marital harmony)
- **TN anchor:** **Thirumanancheri Kalyanasundareswarar Temple**, Kuthalam, Mayiladuthurai
  district — the most-cited temple in Tamil Nadu for removing delays in marriage and for
  marital harmony (Shiva married Parvati here as *Kalyanasundarar*).
  Sources: [Culture & Heritage](https://cultureandheritage.org/thirumanancheri-temple-in-kuthalam-tamil-nadu), [TamilNadu Tourism Info](https://tamilnadutourisminfo.com/?p=6925)
- **Outside TN (to-confirm):** widely cited options include **Tiruvannamalai–region** practices
  and, in North India, temples where *Mangala Gauri* is worshipped (e.g. Gaya, Bihar). Confirm
  specifics before use.
  Source (list, lower reliability): [Culture & Heritage — marriage-delay temples](https://cultureandheritage.org/2024/02/ancient-temples-in-india-where-people-go-to-overcome-delays-in-marriage.html)

### Progeny / children (fertility, safe pregnancy)
- **TN anchor:** **Sri Garbarakshambigai (Mullaivananathar) Temple**, Thirukarukavur, Thanjavur
  district — the classic temple for conceiving and for protection of the womb/pregnancy;
  the goddess's name literally means "protector of the womb."
  Sources: [Hindupedia](https://hindupedia.com/en/Thirukkarugaavoor_Sri_Garbharakshaambigai), [Hindu-blog](https://www.hindu-blog.com/2019/04/garbha-rakshambika-temple-tamil-Nadu-garbarakshambigai-story-ghee-oil.html)
- **Outside TN (to-confirm):** *Santana Gopala* / *Santana Venugopala* Krishna shrines are the
  usual pan-Indian recourse for progeny; confirm a specific well-run temple before recommending.

### Health, healing, longevity
- **TN anchor:** **Vaitheeswaran Koil** (Sri Vaidyanathar), Mayiladuthurai district — "the god
  of healing"; the tank *Siddhamirtham* and traditional offerings are associated with curing
  disease, and it also holds a **Dhanvantari** (divine physician) shrine. Note: it is *also*
  the **Mars (Angaraka) Navagraha** temple, so it doubles as a health and a Mars-remedy site.
  Source: [Wikipedia — Vaitheeswaran Koil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vaitheeswaran_Koil)
- **Outside TN (to-confirm):** **Thottuva Dhanwanthari Temple**, Ernakulam, Kerala — dedicated
  to Dhanvantari, the deity of medicine/health. Confirm before use.

### Wealth, prosperity, finance
- **TN anchors:** **Ashtalakshmi Temple**, Besant Nagar, Chennai (the eight forms of Lakshmi —
  wealth, prosperity); and **Sri Lakshmi Narayani Golden Temple (Sripuram)**, Vellore.
  Source: [Incredible India (Govt.) — Ashtalakshmi Temple](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/tamil-nadu/chennai/ashtalakshmi-temple)
- **Outside TN:** **Kolhapur Mahalakshmi (Ambabai) Temple**, Maharashtra (a Shakti Peetha of
  wealth); **Tirumala Venkateswara**, Andhra Pradesh; and **Padmanabhaswamy**, Thiruvananthapuram,
  Kerala (already on the owner's initial list).
  Source: [Kolhapur Mahalakshmi](https://gokshetra.com/kolhapur-mahalakshmi-temple-story-miracles-powers-legend)

### Education, knowledge, intellect
- **TN anchor:** **Koothanur Maha Saraswathi Amman Temple**, Nannilam, Tiruvarur district — the
  principal Saraswati (goddess of learning) temple in Tamil Nadu, sought for education,
  knowledge and intellect.
  Sources: [South India Tours](https://www.southindiatoursandtravels.com/koothanur-maha-saraswathi-temple), [TamilNadu Tourism Info](https://tamilnadutourisminfo.com/?p=6909)
- **Outside TN:** **Basar Gnana Saraswati Temple**, Telangana; **Sringeri Sharadamba Temple**,
  Karnataka — both major Saraswati seats.

### Career, success, removal of obstacles (note)
- Career success is classically approached through **Sun** (authority — *Suryanar Koil*),
  **Saturn** (discipline/karma — *Thirunallar*), and **Ganesha** for obstacle-removal before
  new ventures — the owner's initial list already includes **Pillaiyarpatti** (Ganesha,
  Karaikudi). A dedicated "career" deity temple is less standardised than the above aspects;
  recommend handling career through the planetary temples in Part B rather than over-claiming a
  single career temple. *To-research further when remedies are unparked.*

## B. Planetary (Navagraha) remedy temples — the backbone

The owner's initial temples sit in this framework (e.g. **Kanjanur** = Venus/Sukran). The nine
classical Navagraha temples cluster around Kumbakonam, Tamil Nadu, and each is the remedy site
for its planet (and thus the life aspects that planet governs):

| Planet | Temple (Tamil Nadu) | Governs (life aspects) |
|---|---|---|
| Sun (Surya) | Suryanar Koil, Kumbakonam | authority, career standing, father, vitality |
| Moon (Chandra) | Thingalur | mind, mother, emotional steadiness |
| Mars (Angaraka) | Vaitheeswaran Koil | drive, siblings, property, health (see above) |
| Mercury (Budha) | Thiruvenkadu | intellect, commerce, speech |
| Jupiter (Guru) | Alangudi | wisdom, wealth, children, guidance |
| Venus (Sukra) | **Kanjanur** *(owner's list)* | marriage, comforts, art, vehicles, trade |
| Saturn (Shani) | Thirunallar | discipline, longevity, career karma, service |
| Rahu | Thirunageswaram | ambition, foreign, sudden change |
| Ketu | Keezhperumpallam | detachment, liberation, hidden gains |

Outside TN, the pan-Indian Saturn recourse is **Shani Shingnapur**, Maharashtra. Rahu/Ketu and
the others are most authentically served by the TN Navagraha set above.
