D1 — Rashi Chart (Birth Chart)
This is your primary birth chart. It shows the actual positions of all 9 planets in the sky at the moment you were born, divided across 12 houses. Think of it as a map of the life you were born into.
D9 — Navamsha Chart (Soul & Maturity Chart)
If D1 is your smartphone hardware, D9 is the operating system — it shapes how the hardware actually performs in real life, especially in your relationships and inner strength. A full reading of how this durability and depth shift — deepening or softening from D1 to D9 — is covered in detail in the paid Life Indicators report.

What is Vimshottari Dasha?

Think of the Mahadasha as the season of your life — like summer or winter. The Antardasha is the month within that season. Your 120-year life span is divided into these planetary seasons.
