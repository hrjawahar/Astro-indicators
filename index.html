D1 — Rashi Chart (Birth Chart)
This is your primary birth chart. It shows the actual positions of all 9 planets in the sky at the moment you were born, divided across 12 houses. Think of it as a map of the life you were born into — the promises, challenges, and natural tendencies written into your existence from the start. D1 is always read first.
D9 — Navamsha Chart (Soul & Maturity Chart)
If D1 is your smartphone hardware, D9 is the operating system — it shapes how the hardware actually performs in real life, especially in your relationships and inner strength. Each sign in D1 is divided into 9 equal parts; your D9 shows which part each planet falls in. If D1 promises something and D9 confirms it, the result is strong and lasting. If D9 contradicts D1, the early promise fades with time.

What is Vimshottari Dasha?

Think of the Mahadasha as the season of your life — like summer or winter. The Antardasha is the month within that season. The planet running the Antardasha colours the events you experience, filtered through the main season's energy. Your 120-year life span is divided into these planetary seasons, each ruled by a different planet whose themes dominate your experience during that time.

Each Maha Dasa (major period) is subdivided into Antar Dasas (sub-periods). The current Antar Dasa shows where the focus is within the broader Maha Dasa theme.
